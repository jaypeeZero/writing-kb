Fiction Cheatsheet
Character · Scene · Plot & Tension
Character
"Action is character."
  • What they do when it costs them something
  • Performance drops → character surfaces
Want vs. Need
  • Want — conscious goal
  • Need — unconscious truth
  • Story lives in the gap between them
Misbelief
  • Load-bearing wrong belief carried into the story
  • Must be one precise sentence: belief + origin + protective behavior
  • "Trust issues" → nothing. "Believes vulnerability will be weaponized because her brother used her secrets against her" → scenes
  • When threatened: character generates their own resistance — may look like rationalization or avoidance, not stubbornness
Test: does this scene threaten the misbelief? If no → scene is inert.
Reveal
  • Choice under pressure = X-ray. Breaking point, not success.
  • Omission — what they won't say reveals as much as action
  • One precise detail > paragraph of description
Quick Checks
  • What do they want? What do they actually need?
  • Is the misbelief precise enough to dictate behavior when threatened?
  • What will they never say?
  • Remove name & description — known by choices alone?
Scene
Smallest unit that does work.
Nothing changes → cut it.
Three Bones
  • Goal — character wants something specific (not vague)
  • Resistance — something opposes it
  • Turn — situation or understanding different at the end
Beat Shape
Goal → Friction → Escalate → Crisis → Choice → Resolution
  • Crisis = two bad options (or two good ones that exclude each other)
  • Each beat raises stakes or narrows options — never same intensity twice
Endings — Not Success
  • Fail to get what they wanted
  • Get it — but it costs something
  • Get it — discover it wasn't what they needed
  • Worse new problem emerges from the attempt
  • Question raised remains unanswered
Crucible & Subtext
  • Crucible: characters can't leave — physical trap or motivation > exit cost
  • Subtext: gap between said and meant. Every character has a different agenda.
  • Dialogue = trying to get what you want from someone, not exchanging info
Quick Checks
  • What does the character want?
  • What's stopping them?
  • What's different at the end?
  • Why can't they leave?
  • Does this scene's outcome force the next one?
Plot & Tension
Plot = causal chain.
  • Scene outcome → next scene's input
  • Scenes rearrangeable = episodes, not plot
Tension
Question + withheld answer.
Not action. Not conflict.
  • Dramatic irony — reader knows what character doesn't
  • Ticking clock — any resource running out
  • Constraint — can't-act generates more tension than action
  • Uncertainty — certainty kills tension
Pacing
  • Scene = real time → moments that change things
  • Summary = compressed → transitions, routines
  • Page time ∝ emotional weight
  • Climax = max expansion. Resolution = compression.
Short sentences = fast · Long = slow · Solo ¶ = brake
Avoid
  • Telegraphing — "she didn't know it was the last time"
  • Naming tension — "the room was tense"
  • False tension — reader knows the character is fine
  • Constant max tension — nothing has weight
  • Stakes without investment — reader doesn't care
Quick Checks
  • What question is the reader holding right now?
  • What does the reader know that the character doesn't?
  • What can't this character do or say?
  • What moments deserve the most page time — getting it?
